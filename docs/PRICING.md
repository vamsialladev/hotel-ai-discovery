## Pricing options

1. **Fixed-fee pilot.** Build plus three months of run and support. The client knows the total up front; I carry
   the build-overrun risk.
2. **Per property per month.** Covers run cost and support with the target margin. Simple for a group adding
   properties one at a time.
3. **Group licence per year.** Twelve months of the per-property price for every property, 20% off. Rewards
   committing the whole group.

## What this shows

- **Model tokens are the smallest cost.** At the measured rates, tokens for both apps are a few dollars a month
  for two properties. Support time is the main cost, so it's what the price should track.
- **The per-property price falls with scale** because hosting and support are shared. At 100 properties the
  monthly price is close to cost plus margin on support.
- **Against projected value:** the stage 1 and stage 2 business cases project about $69,000 a year net value for
  two properties in the base case ($31,887 + $37,052). The base per-property price is about 16% of that. Those
  value numbers are projections too.
